About Us

Cosplay & Costume Design Club brings together a strong community of UTK students who share a passion for dressing up as characters from their favorite anime, comics, movies, TV shows, and so much more! Join us for hangouts/activities, all are welcome! We meet every Friday in Strong 103 from 6-8 pm, with meetings consisting of sewing, wig-styling, prop-making, and many other costume-making lessons, as well as providing information about cosplay and conventions as a whole! We also host hangouts, workshops, demos, and more! All skill levels welcome. Feel free to join our Discord for more info, or follow our Instagram for weekly updates!

Our mission

Our mission is to create a welcoming environment for students with interests in cosplay, costume design, performance, and other related hobbies, along with providing a space for building connections and friendships through a focus on fostering and developing creative skills involved with the hobbies aforementioned.
